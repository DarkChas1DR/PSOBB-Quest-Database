# Observed quest counts

These are maxima in the supplied 527 quest variants, **not safe limits**. Counts include only placements classified as monsters. NPCs and unknown types are separate in the JSON. Child entities and random-generated enemies are not expanded. Wave zero is included. Distinct wave IDs do not prove a sequence is reachable or active.
