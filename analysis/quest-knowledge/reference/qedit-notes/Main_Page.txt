Welcome to the Official QEdit Wiki Guide

If you would like to make your own quests for Phantasy Star Online first you'll need to download QEdit (https://qedit.info/uploads/qedit.rar). This is the full package for Qedit, you can find more updated exes on our discord. QEdit was developed by Schthack and Co. But due to links going dead the download is hosted here to ensure preservation. Latest Qedit version (https://github.com/Alisaryn/qedit/releases/download/v2.0c/QeditUni.exe)

Here you'll be able to lookup OPCodes and their use, and more importantly how to use. Feel free to register, however to be able to contribute to this site please contact Kayak on discord to get your account verified.

Feel free to join our discord (https://discord.gg/Ww2G9Zb)
